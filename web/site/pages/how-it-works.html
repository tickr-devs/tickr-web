How it works

Seven checks
before any order.

Every trade takes the same path, in the same order, every time. Where the path depends on you, it uses your settings. Where it protects your money, it does not bend.

The path of a trade

From thousands of stocks to one order with a stop.

  1. 01Screenersautomatic

    The bot reads the market's lists of the most active stocks of the moment, from before the open to the close.

  2. 02Universeyour settings

    Only stocks inside your price range, volume and spread settings stay on the list. Everything else is ignored for the day.

  3. 03Modelyour settings

    Your personal model looks at each stock and decides whether this is an entry. It was trained for your settings, and you saw how it would have done on past days before it traded.

  4. 04Riskyour settings

    Position size, the daily loss limit including fees, and the number of open positions. If an entry would cross any of them, it does not happen.

  5. 05Orderfixed

    The order is written down first, then sent exactly once. If the answer is unclear, the bot asks the broker instead of sending again.

  6. 06Broker stopfixed

    A stop is placed at your broker and confirmed. Only then does the bot count the position as open. Without a confirmed stop, there is no position.

  7. 07Reconcilefixed

    All day, the bot compares its records with your broker's. If they differ, new entries stop until they match, and you get a notice.

You and the bot

You choose the limits. The bot keeps them.

You decide
  • How much goes into one position
  • How much the bot may lose in a day, fees included
  • Which sessions it trades: pre-market, regular, after-hours
  • The price range and how liquid a stock must be
  • Paper trading or live trading
  • When to stop it. One tap, any time
Always the same
  • A stop at your broker for every position
  • One send per decision, never an automatic retry
  • Your broker's records win over the bot's
  • Flat by the end of your last session
  • Loosening a protective setting needs Face ID, waits until the next session and sends you an email

A trading day

On when the market is, off when it is not.

The bot runs only in the sessions you turn on. By the end of the last one, every position is closed and the day's records are checked against the broker.

The main session. On by default.
