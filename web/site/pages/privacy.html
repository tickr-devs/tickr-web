Privacy

Website and launch list

Updated November 27, 2026

What this covers

This page covers the tickr.bot website and its launch list. The product is not released yet; it will get its own privacy notice before it is.

What we collect

  • The email address you type into the form.
  • The language the page was shown in, so our email comes in that language.
  • When you signed up and when you confirmed your address.

That is all. The site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or ads.

What we use it for

To confirm your address, and then one email when the beta opens. Nothing else. We never sell, rent or share the list.

Where it is kept

In our own database on our own server. It is not handed to advertisers or data brokers.

Spam protection

To stop automated sign-ups, the server limits how often one connection can submit the form. For that it uses the IP address briefly and does not keep it with your email.

Fonts

The site uses your device’s own fonts and loads nothing from other companies.

Leaving the list

Every email from us has a link to leave the list. You can also write to hello@tickr.bot from the address you signed up with. We delete it and confirm by reply.

Questions

Anything about your data: hello@tickr.bot.
