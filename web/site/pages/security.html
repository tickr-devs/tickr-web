Security

Your key goes in once.
Only your bot can read it.

tickr.bot trades through an access key from your own brokerage account. Everything about the design starts from one question: what if someone tried to misuse that key?

The key

Stored encrypted, apart from everything else.

01 · YouPaste the key once

On the website, over an encrypted connection. It is checked and never shown again.

02 · VaultEncrypted, kept apart

In a separate vault, not in the database with your account.

03 · Your botThe only reader

Your bot runs in its own isolated space and is the only thing that can open your key.

04 · Everyone elseNo access

Not the website, not the apps, not other users' bots, not our team.

Your account

Nothing to steal, a lot to get through.

Sign-inNo password

A one-time code by email, or Sign in with Apple or Google. Nothing to guess or reuse.

Second stepPasskey or code

Face ID, Touch ID or an authenticator code. Always available, required for live trading.

DevicesSign out everywhere

See every signed-in device. Lost your phone? Remove it and it sees nothing more.

AppsNo broker key on your phone

The apps keep only a session token in the system keychain. They talk to your bot, never to your broker.

Protective settingsThree locks to loosen

Face ID, a wait until the next session, and an email to you. Tightening is instant.

Your own tradingYours stays yours

Trade by hand on the same account if you like. The bot asks "was it you?" only when something touches its own positions or stops.

Never

What the bot never does.

  1. 01

    Moves money.

    It places and cancels stock orders. Deposits, withdrawals and transfers are not something it can ask your broker for.

  2. 02

    Holds a position without a stop.

    The stop is at your broker. If the bot, our servers or your phone go quiet, it is still there.

  3. 03

    Sends an order twice.

    Every order is written down before it leaves. An unclear answer means asking the broker, not sending again.

  4. 04

    Keeps trading on a mismatch.

    If your broker's records and the bot's disagree, new entries stop until they match, and you are told.

  5. 05

    Holds overnight.

    Every position is closed by the end of your last session.

On your side

Two things that make a key safer.

Limit the key to our servers

When you create the key, your broker lets you list the addresses that may use it. We show you ours. A copied key then does not work anywhere else.

Turn it off in one place

If anything ever looks wrong, revoke the key at your broker. The bot stops trading at once, and the stops already at your broker stay in place.
