  1. Live Activity

    Positions, today's result and how much of the loss limit is used, on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island while the market is open.

  2. Alerts you choose

    Entries, exits, stops, pauses and the end of the day. Turn each one on or off. Anything that needs you is never silent.

  3. One tap to stop

    Stop the bot, close a position or close everything, from your pocket. Stops at your broker stay in place until positions are closed.

  4. Face ID for what matters

    Loosening a protective setting asks for Face ID, takes effect at the next session and sends you an email.
