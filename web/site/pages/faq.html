What people ask before they join. Missing something? Write to hello@tickr.bot.

What is tickr.bot?

Software that trades US stocks intraday in your own brokerage account, inside the limits you set. It runs on our servers; you watch it in the Mac and iPhone apps.

Is this investment advice?

No. tickr.bot is software. You choose every setting it trades by, and the decisions about your money are yours.

Will it make money?

Nobody can promise that, and we do not. Before your model trades you see how it would have done on past days, and you start in paper trading. Past results do not guarantee future results.

Which brokers work?

At launch, one US broker with an official trading API. We will name it when the beta opens. More may follow.

What does it trade?

US common stocks, intraday. It picks from the most active stocks of the moment that fit your price, volume and spread settings. No options, futures, crypto or overnight positions.

Can I still trade by hand on the same account?

Yes. Your account stays yours. The bot only asks "was it you?" when something touches its own positions or stops.

What if my phone, the app or your servers go offline?

Every position has a stop at your broker, so it is protected even if everything on our side goes quiet. The bot runs on our servers, not on your phone.

Can tickr.bot withdraw my money?

No. It places and cancels stock orders. Deposits, withdrawals and transfers are not something it can ask your broker for. See Security.

How much will it cost?

Prices are not set yet; we will publish them before the beta opens. There will be a free trial, and we will never take a percentage of your profit.

When can I use it?

The closed beta opens first, in paper trading. Leave your email and we will write once, when your place is ready.
