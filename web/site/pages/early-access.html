From the list to your first day

Six steps, about an hour of your time.

  1. 01Invitation

    One email when your place in the beta is ready.

  2. 02Account

    Sign up with an email code, Apple or Google, and accept the terms: it is software, and the risk is yours.

  3. 03Paper account

    Connect the paper trading account at your broker. Step-by-step instructions with pictures, and the key is checked at once.

  4. 04Settings

    A short set: position size, daily loss limit, sessions, price range. Each has a sensible default and a plain explanation.

  5. 05Your model

    Your model is trained for your settings. When it is ready, you see how it would have done on past days: earned, lost and net.

  6. 06First day

    Install the apps, sign in with the same account and start the bot in paper trading.

Live trading comes later, as a separate step you take on purpose: a second sign-in factor, a separate key and an explicit confirmation of risk.

Pricing

Simple, and never a share of your profit.

Prices are not set yet. We will publish them before the beta opens. Three things are already decided.

01A free trial

Try it in paper trading before you pay anything.

02No cut of profits

You pay for the software and the time it runs. Never a percentage of what you make.

03Stops stay, always

If a payment lapses, the bot stops opening trades. Stops at your broker stay until positions are closed.

Who it is for

Self-directed traders with their own US brokerage account.

A good fit
  • You trade US stocks yourself and want the routine automated
  • You want limits that hold even when you are not watching
  • You are happy to start in paper trading
Not a fit
  • You want someone to choose investments for you
  • You want positions held for days or weeks
  • You cannot afford to lose the money you trade with
